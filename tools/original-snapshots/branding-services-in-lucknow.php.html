FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our branding services in Lucknow.

What is included in a brand identity package?
Our comprehensive brand identity package typically includes logo design, typography selection, color palettes, brand guidelines (a rulebook for your visual style), and essential stationery design like business cards and letterheads.
How long does the branding process take?
A complete branding project usually takes between 3 to 6 weeks. This timeline ensures we have adequate time for thorough research, multiple design concepts, client feedback loops, and final polishing.
Can you rebrand an existing business without losing its current customers?
Absolutely. We specialize in strategic rebranding that honors your company's heritage while modernizing its appeal. We carefully manage the transition to excite your existing customer base while attracting a new audience.
Do I really need a brand guideline document?
Yes. A brand guideline document is crucial. It ensures that no matter who works on your marketing or internal materials in the future, your brand remains consistent, professional, and easily recognizable across all platforms.
How much does branding cost?
The investment varies depending on the scale and deliverables required—from a simple logo design to a completely comprehensive corporate identity package. We provide a detailed, custom quote after an initial discovery session.
What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?
A logo is just one visual mark that identifies your business. A brand identity encompasses your logo, color palette, typography, visual style, voice, and the overall feeling you project to the world.
Do you also provide physical marketing materials like business cards?
Yes, our branding services seamlessly extend into tangible marketing materials including business cards, letterheads, brochures, flex banners, and comprehensive packaging design.
Will we get different formats of our logo once completed?
Absolutely. We provide your final brand assets in all standard file formats (like SVG, AI, PNG, and PDF) ensuring you have exactly what's needed for both high-quality print and digital web use.
