FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our web development services and processes.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices today. We employ a mobile-first design approach, ensuring your website is fully responsive and looks fantastic on smartphones, tablets, and desktops alike.

How much does a new website cost?

The cost varies depending on the complexity, features, and number of pages required. A simple corporate site differs in cost from a fully custom e-commerce web application. Contact us for a free, detailed, and transparent quote tailored to your specific needs.

How long does it take to build a website?

Typically, a standard corporate website takes around 3 to 6 weeks from strategy to launch. More complex e-commerce or custom applications can take 2 to 3 months. We provide clear timelines during the discovery phase and stick to them.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

Yes! We build most of our websites using powerful Content Management Systems (CMS) like WordPress. We provide full training upon launch, giving you the ability to easily add blog posts, update images, and change text without touching a single line of code.

Do you provide SEO services along with web development?

Yes. All our websites are built with strict on-page SEO best practices in mind—including fast load times, semantic HTML tags, XML sitemaps, and mobile responsiveness. For ongoing ranking improvements, we also offer dedicated Monthly SEO packages.

Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Definitely. We offer comprehensive maintenance packages that include regular software updates, security scanning, daily backups, uptime monitoring, and priority technical support to keep your investment secure and performing optimally.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, we specialize in website revamps. We will analyze your current site's performance, preserve your existing SEO rankings, and overhaul the design and architecture to meet modern web standards and improve conversion rates.

Do you offer e-commerce website development?

Yes, we build robust, secure, and scalable e-commerce platforms with custom features, seamless payment gateway integrations, and intuitive inventory management systems.
