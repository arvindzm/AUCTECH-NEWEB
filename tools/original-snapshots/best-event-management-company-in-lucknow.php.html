Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our event management and planning services in Lucknow.

What types of events do you manage in Lucknow?
We manage a wide spectrum of events including corporate conferences, product launches, trade exhibitions, brand activations, marathons and sporting events, gala dinners, anniversary parties, and social celebrations of all scales.
Do you offer end-to-end event management?
Yes. Our service is fully comprehensive — from initial concept development and venue scouting to vendor management, on-site coordination, and post-event analysis. You can rely on us to manage 100% of your event.
How far in advance should I book an event planner?
For large-scale events like corporate conferences or exhibitions, we recommend booking at least 2–3 months in advance. For smaller events, 4–6 weeks is generally sufficient. However, we also handle urgent bookings — contact us and we'll assess feasibility.
Can you help find and book event venues in Lucknow?
Absolutely. We have a strong network of premium venues across Lucknow — from 5-star banquet halls and open-air lawns to convention centers and corporate auditoriums. We find the perfect venue that matches your event type and budget.
Do you manage event decoration and theming?
Yes. Our creative team designs and executes complete event décor — from branded stage setups and floral arrangements to immersive themed environments. We work with trusted décor vendors to make every space visually stunning.
How do you handle last-minute changes or emergencies during an event?
Our team always prepares a backup contingency plan before every event. We have on-ground coordinators, standby vendor alternatives, and a real-time communication protocol to handle any unexpected situation swiftly and invisibly.
Do you provide live event photography and videography?
Yes. Through our in-house production capabilities, we can provide professional photography, videography, live streaming, and same-day highlight reel creation for your event, ensuring every important moment is captured.
How is an event management cost typically calculated?
Event management fees depend on the event scale, number of guests, complexity, and required services. We provide a completely transparent, itemized quotation after the initial consultation so you understand exactly where every rupee is allocated.
