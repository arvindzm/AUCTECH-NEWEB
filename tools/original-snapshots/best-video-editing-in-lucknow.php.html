Our Streamlined Production Process

01

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our video editing and production services.

What video editing software do you use?
We use industry-standard professional software including Adobe Premiere Pro for video editing, DaVinci Resolve for colour grading, Adobe After Effects for motion graphics, and Adobe Audition for sound design and mixing.
How long does it take to edit a video?
Turnaround depends on the project complexity. A short-form reel (under 60 seconds) is typically delivered within 24–48 hours. A full corporate video or YouTube video (5–10 minutes) takes 3–5 business days. We also offer express delivery for urgent projects.
What formats will I receive the final video in?
We deliver the final video in all formats you require — 4K MP4 for YouTube, web-compressed MP4 for websites, vertical 9:16 format for Reels/TikTok, and broadcast-quality ProRes or H.264 for TV and presentations.
Do you provide voiceover or subtitles with the video?
Yes! We offer professional voiceover recording in Hindi and English, as well as SRT subtitle files and burned-in captions. We can also add auto-animated subtitles for social media content.
How many revisions are included?
We include up to 3 revision rounds in every project. Our goal is your complete satisfaction, so we refine the edit based on your feedback until the video perfectly matches your brand and vision.
Can you edit videos for YouTube monetization and SEO?
Absolutely. We optimize every YouTube video for maximum watch-time — the primary metric for monetization and ranking. We add engaging hooks, dynamic pacing, end screens, chapters, and descriptive on-screen text that boost both retention and discoverability.
What raw footage formats do you accept?
We accept virtually all camera formats — MP4, MOV, AVI, MXF, RAW, R3D, BRAW, ProRes, and more. Whether shot on a DSLR, smartphone, Cinema Camera, or GoPro, we can work with your footage.
Do you also create motion graphics and animations for videos?
Yes. Our editors are also skilled motion designers. We create lower thirds, kinetic typography, animated logos, infographic animations, and custom transitions — everything to make your video stand above the competition.
