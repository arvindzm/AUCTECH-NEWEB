FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our 3D walkthrough services.

What is a 3D architectural walkthrough?
A 3D architectural walkthrough is a computer-generated, photorealistic animation or interactive tour that allows you to explore a building or space virtually — as if you were physically walking through it. It's used by architects, real estate developers, and interior designers to visualize projects before construction begins.
How much does a 3D walkthrough cost in Lucknow?
The cost depends on the project size, complexity, and output format. At Auctech IT Solutions, our 3D walkthrough services start from ₹15,000 for basic projects. We offer competitive pricing and flexible packages to suit all budgets. Contact us for a free, customized quote.
How long does it take to create a 3D walkthrough?
Typically, a standard 3D walkthrough takes 5-10 business days depending on the project's complexity and scope. Urgent projects can be fast-tracked with our express delivery option. We always ensure timely delivery without compromising on quality.
What files do you need to start a 3D walkthrough project?
We typically need your floor plans (AutoCAD or PDF), elevation drawings, material/color preferences, and any reference images. If you don't have detailed blueprints, don't worry — our team can work with sketches or rough ideas to create your visualization.
Can I make changes after seeing the initial 3D walkthrough?
Absolutely! We include a revision round in every project. After the initial draft, you can request changes to materials, colors, camera angles, lighting, and more. Our goal is to ensure you're 100% satisfied with the final output.
Do you provide VR-compatible 3D walkthroughs?
Yes! We create VR-ready walkthroughs compatible with Oculus, HTC Vive, and web-based VR viewers. This allows your clients to experience the property in an immersive, 360-degree environment — perfect for real estate showrooms and exhibitions.
Why should I choose Auctech IT Solutions for 3D walkthroughs in Lucknow?
With 10+ years of experience, 500+ completed projects, and a dedicated team of 3D artists and architects, Auctech IT Solutions is Lucknow's most trusted 3D walkthrough company. We combine cutting-edge technology with creative mastery to deliver results that consistently exceed expectations.
Do you add background music and voiceovers to the walkthrough?
Yes, all our cinematic 3D walkthroughs can be enhanced with professional background scores, sound effects, and multilingual voiceovers to create a truly immersive and emotional experience for your clients.
