Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Insights regarding our engineering timelines, costs, and software capabilities.

What technologies do you use for software development?
At Auctech IT Solutions, we utilize modern and highly stable tech stacks. Our backend expertise includes PHP (Laravel/CodeIgniter), Node.js, and Python, while our frontend spans React, Vue.js, and angular. We deploy on top-tier cloud providers like AWS and Google Cloud to ensure 99.9% uptime.
Will I own the source code of my custom software?
Absolutely. Once the project is fully completed and outstanding invoices are cleared, we transfer 100% intellectual property rights and the complete source code to your business as per our contract terms.
How much does custom software development cost?
The cost entirely depends on the complexity, scale, and features required. A simple billing software will cost significantly less than an enterprise-grade Hospital Management System. We provide free, transparent, and highly detailed scopes of work and pricing estimates before you commit.
Can you integrate my existing tools and databases?
Yes. A critical part of our service involves securely bridging new software with your legacy systems, payment gateways, and third-party APIs (like WhatsApp API, MailChimp, existing ERP solutions, etc.) to ensure a seamless operational flow.
Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?
Yes, we provide continuous technical support, bug tracking, feature scaling, and general maintenance packages (SLAs). We don’t just launch your software; we make sure it runs optimally 24/7/365.
Does custom software development take a long time?
Developing custom software depends on the scale. An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) can take 2-3 months, while complex enterprise suites can take 6 months. We break down the project into agile milestones for early deliverables.
Can you upgrade my existing legacy software?
Yes. We can refactor existing code, optimize database performance, migrate your legacy systems to the cloud, and modernize outdated interfaces without losing historic data.
Will my custom software be mobile compatible?
We specialize in building responsive web applications that work perfectly on mobile browsers, as well as developing dedicated native or cross-platform mobile apps for iOS and Android.
