Frequently Asked Questions

We answer common questions about SEO, Ads, and the impact of digital marketing on local businesses.

Why is Auctech IT Solutions the best digital marketing agency in Lucknow?
We blend technical expertise with creative problem-solving. Unlike agencies that offer generic packages, we build custom funnels integrating deep SEO, high-conversion PPC, and aggressive social media tactics focused purely on measurable ROI.
How long does SEO take to produce results?
SEO is a long-term strategy. While some local improvements can be seen in 4 to 6 weeks, it generally takes 3 to 6 months to secure competitive, first-page rankings that generate consistent organic traffic and leads.
What is the difference between SEO and PPC?
SEO focuses on earning free organic traffic by ranking high on search engines through optimization. PPC (Pay-Per-Click) involves paying for immediate top-page ad placements on Google or social media, granting instant visibility.
Can a small local business benefit from digital marketing?
Absolutely. Local SEO and targeted social media ads are incredibly powerful for small businesses, allowing you to dominate local map packs (Google My Business) and reach customers in your exact city or zip code efficiently.
Do you optimize content for AI and Voice Search (AEO & GEO)?
Yes. Modern search is evolving. We utilize Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) by creating structured, conversational content rich with Schema markup so AI models and voice assistants accurately recommend your business.
How much should I budget for digital marketing?
Your budget depends on your market competitiveness and aggressiveness. We offer flexible retainers and always suggest a budget that ensures positive ROI based on your specific industry's cost-per-acquisition metrics.
Do you provide monthly progress reports?
Yes. Transparency is our core value. You receive detailed monthly performance reports outlining key metrics, traffic growth, leads generated, ad spend breakdowns, and strategy adjustments for the next cycle.
Which social media platforms should my business use?
It depends on your target audience. B2B companies excel on LinkedIn and Twitter, while visually-driven or B2C brands do better on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. We analyze your demographic to determine the best platform.
