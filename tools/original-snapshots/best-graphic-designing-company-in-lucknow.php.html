What are the benefits of hiring a professional graphic designer?
Professional graphic designers possess the skills and experience to create visuals that are strategically effective — not just aesthetically pleasing. They develop a cohesive visual identity that resonates with your target audience, builds brand trust, and drives measurable business results.
How much does graphic design cost in Lucknow?
Pricing varies by project scope. Logo design starts from ₹5,000, social media packages from ₹3,000/month, and complete brand identity projects from ₹15,000. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your specific requirements.
What software do you use for graphic design?
We use industry-standard tools including Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and Figma. For motion graphics, we utilize After Effects. This ensures maximum quality and compatibility across all output formats.
How long does it take to design a logo?
A standard logo design project typically takes 5-10 business days, including concept development, client feedback rounds, and final revisions. Rush delivery is available for urgent projects at an additional cost.
Do you offer revisions?
Absolutely! We include multiple revision rounds in every project. We incorporate your feedback on colors, layouts, typography, and details until you are 100% satisfied with the final design output.
Can you create designs for both print and digital?
Yes! We deliver files optimized for both print (CMYK, 300 DPI, bleed marks) and digital (RGB, web-optimized, social media dimensions). You'll receive all necessary file formats for every use case.
